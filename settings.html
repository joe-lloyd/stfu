Storage

0 means no limit. Days older than the limit are removed entirely. Over the size limit, the oldest recordings go first and their transcripts stay, since audio is nearly all of the space. You get a warning at 80% of the size limit. With "ask me" on, nothing is ever deleted until you confirm here; off, cleanup runs by itself after each dictation.

Profile

A profile is a whole set of providers. Keep a cloud one for everyday speed and a fully local one for anything sensitive, then switch here or from the tray menu. Settings below always edit the selected profile.
